Why visceral fat matters

It hides inside

Visceral fat wraps the liver, intestines and other organs. You can carry a lot of it without looking overweight, which is why scans matter more than the mirror.

It's metabolically active

Research links it to insulin resistance, inflammation, fatty liver and heart disease more strongly than the fat just under the skin.

It responds to food

Visceral fat often shrinks faster than other fat when diet changes. Early studies suggest certain probiotic strains and fermented foods may help.
