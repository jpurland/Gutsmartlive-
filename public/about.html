About Gut Smart Live

Gut Smart Live is an independent resource for people who want to eat living, fermented foods with every meal and understand what the science says about visceral fat.

The idea was sparked by Dr. Sean O'Mara, who uses MRI scans to show how visceral fat changes with diet and recommends eating fermented foods alongside meat at the same meal. We take a broader view: any protein, any traditional living food, and a running log of the published research. Gut Smart Live is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dr. O'Mara.

Where the research comes from

Every morning the site searches PubMed, the database run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine, for new studies on probiotics, fermented foods and visceral fat. New titles are logged on the research page and link straight to the source. Our articles explain the strongest findings in plain language and say clearly where the evidence is still thin.

How stories are reviewed

Every member story and comment is read before it appears. We post experiences, good and bad, and remove anything that sells a product, gives risky medical instructions or shares someone else's private details.

Not medical advice

Everything on this site is for education. Fermented foods aren't right for everyone. People who are immune-compromised, pregnant, on a low-sodium or low-histamine diet, or managing a health condition should talk with a doctor first.

Gut Smart Live is an independent resource on fermented, probiotic-rich foods and visceral fat. It is educational only and not medical advice. Talk with your doctor before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, immune-compromised or managing a health condition.
