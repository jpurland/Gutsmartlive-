Type 2 Diabetes

People with type 2 diabetes tend to carry fewer fiber-fermenting, butyrate-producing gut bacteria, and the most common diabetes drug, metformin, appears to work partly through the gut. Human trials show that diet changes the gut in ways that can help blood sugar, but the gut is one lever among several and supports medical care rather than replacing it.
